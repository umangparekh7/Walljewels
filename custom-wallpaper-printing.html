5 Architectural Substrate Finishes

Non-Woven · ₹120 / sq.ft Soft · Premium · Breathable

Dimensionally stable, paste-the-wall installation, matte non-reflective finish that flatters intricate artwork in bedrooms and living spaces.

HD PVC · ₹120 / sq.ft Durable · Scrubbable · Washable

High-density washable vinyl engineered for high-traffic corridors, children's rooms, dining areas, and commercial retail walls.

Canvas · ₹140 / sq.ft Gallery Texture · Painterly Face

Woven texture that gives large-scale murals the presence of a museum-grade stretched canvas painting.

Sandstone · ₹160 / sq.ft Mineral Relief · Tactile Shadows

Granular mineral surface that catches directional lighting, creating realistic shadow play for stone, temple, and architectural designs.

Canvas Fabric · ₹160 / sq.ft Textile Face · Acoustic Softness

Luxurious textile substrate that softens room acoustics and elevates master suites, home theatres, and executive boardrooms.
