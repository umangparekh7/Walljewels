Frequently Asked Questions — Chennai

How does custom wallpaper printing work in Chennai?

Send us your wall's width and height in feet or inches. Our design studio sends you a scaled digital 3D mockup showing how the artwork sits on your wall. Once approved, we print and schedule installation within 48 to 72 hours.

What is the wallpaper cost per sq.ft in Chennai?

Prices start at ₹120/sq.ft + GST for Non-Woven and HD PVC, ₹140/sq.ft for Canvas, and ₹160/sq.ft for Sandstone and Canvas Fabric. Pricing includes bespoke digital rescaling.

Which areas in Chennai do you service?

We service all areas across Chennai including Anna Nagar, Nungambakkam, Boat Club, Poes Garden, ECR, OMR, Velachery, Adyar, Besant Nagar, Kilpauk, and greater Tamil Nadu.
