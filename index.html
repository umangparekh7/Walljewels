SINCE 1978 · CHENNAI

About Wall Jewels

Pioneers of decorative wallcoverings in South India — transforming interior spaces for nearly five decades.

Founded in 1978 in Chennai (formerly Madras) by visionary entrepreneur Mr. Mukesh Parekh, Wall Jewels Wallpaper World (WJWP) has been at the forefront of transforming interior spaces for nearly five decades. At a time when wallpapers were virtually unknown in India, Mr. Parekh introduced the concept of decorative wallcoverings to the country, laying the foundation for one of India’s most enduring and respected names in the wallpaper industry.

What began as a bold vision to redefine interior décor has grown into a trusted brand serving homeowners, architects, interior designers, hospitality projects and commercial spaces across South India. Through continuous innovation, uncompromising quality and an unwavering commitment to craftsmanship, WJWP has played a significant role in shaping how walls are designed and experienced in modern Indian interiors.

Over the years, the company established a strong retail presence with flagship showrooms across Parrys, T. Nagar and OMR in Chennai, offering one of the region’s most comprehensive collections of premium wallpapers, customised wall murals and interior surface solutions. From timeless classics and heritage-inspired designs to contemporary luxury collections, every wallpaper is carefully curated to complement diverse architectural and interior styles.

As customer expectations evolved, WJWP invested in state-of-the-art in-house digital printing and manufacturing facilities, enabling complete control over design, colour accuracy, material quality and production timelines for bespoke residential and large-scale commercial projects.

Today, WJWP continues to blend traditional artistry with modern technology through exclusive collections such as Kala Parampara and Kala Rasa, transforming ordinary walls into immersive works of art combining aesthetics, craftsmanship and storytelling.

“To inspire beautiful spaces through exceptional design, uncompromising quality and a passion for innovation.”

— Founding Philosophy established in 1978 by Mr. Mukesh Parekh
