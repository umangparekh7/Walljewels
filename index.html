The Wall Jewels journal

Notes from forty-five years of putting art on walls — practical, opinionated, short.

Edition 01

Wallpaper vs paint — the honest ledger

Paint is a colour. Wallpaper is a picture, a texture and a story — and modern vinyl outlives most paint jobs. We still have walls from 2012 on our books, wiped clean with a damp cloth.

Keep reading

The real arithmetic: a repaint every three to four years against one wall that holds a decade. Stains wipe off scrubbable vinyl; hairline plaster cracks vanish behind non-woven backing. And no paint, at any price, can put a Pichwai grove behind your dining table.

Edition 02

How custom wallpaper actually works

Two measurements start it: width and height, in feet. Our studio then recomposes the artwork to your wall — moving a tree, lifting a horizon — so nothing important lands behind a cupboard.

Keep reading

The design is proofed to you on your own wall photograph, printed in-house on the finish you chose, cut into registered panels, and fitted by our crew in an afternoon. If there is active seepage we say so before we quote — wallpaper deserves a dry wall, and honesty keeps it beautiful.

Edition 03

Pichwai on your wall — heritage without imitation

Pichwai was painted to hang behind the deity; its geometry is built for devotion at room scale. That is exactly why it works behind a dining table or a bed — it was always wall art.

Keep reading

Our plates keep the Nathdwara grammar — garlands, cows, lotus ponds — and re-balance the composition for furniture lines and switch plates. Deep indigo grounds take warm lamplight beautifully; give the wall a picture light and the gold begins to move.
