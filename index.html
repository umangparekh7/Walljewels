How it works

Four steps. One afternoon.

Talk it through

Walk into the Parry’s showroom or send photos of the wall. Our consultancy is free — including the honest “don’t do that” advice.

Choose or create

Pick from 300+ catalogues and 50,000 ready-stock designs, or hand us an image and we’ll print it to your exact dimensions.

We measure & check

A site visit confirms square footage and wall condition. Damp is fine; active leakage is not — we’ll tell you before you pay.

Fitted in four hours

A two-person crew covers 400 sq.ft, clears up, and leaves. Free after-sales service if anything lifts.

Since 1978

We were selling wallpaper before most of our competitors existed.

Mukesh M Parekh founded Wall Jewels in 1978, when a patterned wall in a Chennai home was a genuinely radical idea. Three branches, forty people and one 5,000 sq.ft showroom at Parry’s later, we’re still the sole distributor and stockist for Indian wallpapers across South India — and the first Indian wallpaper company to put its catalogue online.
